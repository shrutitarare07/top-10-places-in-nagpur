Nagpur (Orange city)

Nagpur, popularly known as the Orange City, is a fascinating destination in Maharashtra with a blend of history, spirituality, culture and nature. Whether you're visiting for a weekend, travelling with family, or exploring Nagpur for the first time, there are plenty of places worth discovering.
